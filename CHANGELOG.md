# Changelog

## [1.0.0] — 2026-11-23

The first release, as a beta.

- Garages built in game: public, job, gang, private and impound lots
- The standard GTA garages come already placed, with the usual names
- Walk up and press E to open a garage, or use your target script. Each garage can pick its own
- No attendant at the desks by default, just a marker. Attendants can be switched on in the settings
- A garage screen with a picture of every car and its condition
- A brighter garage screen with a clear colour for every car's status
- Parked cars can stay visible in their bays
- Job and gang motor pools by rank, borrowed or bought
- Private garages to buy or rent, with keys for friends
- Police impound with fees, hold times and reasons
- Missing cars go back to their garage or the impound lot
- About 90 settings changed live in the builder
- Works with XS-Housing, XS-PoliceSystem and XS-Phone
